# AI Context

## Project

CodeCompass is a personal education and mentorship brand focused on helping software engineers prepare for technical interviews through structured learning.

The repository is the single source of truth for everything related to the CodeCompass brand including website content, presentations, educational material, interview preparation resources, mentorship assets and future products.

When in doubt, optimize for clarity, simplicity and long-term maintainability rather than flashy design or unnecessary complexity.

---

# Brand Identity

CodeCompass stands for:

- Structured preparation over random practice
- Understanding over memorization
- Consistency over intensity
- Practical guidance over theory
- Clarity over information overload

The brand should feel like an experienced mentor providing direction, not an influencer selling motivation.

Avoid exaggerated marketing language, artificial urgency, or unnecessary hype.

---

# Writing Style

All content should be:

- Professional
- Friendly
- Confident
- Practical
- Beginner-friendly
- Concise

Avoid:

- Buzzwords
- Clickbait titles
- Excessive emojis
- Corporate jargon
- Walls of text

Prefer short paragraphs, clean spacing and clear hierarchy.

---

# Visual Identity

Overall feel:

- Clean
- Modern
- Minimal
- Premium
- Calm
- High readability

The design should feel closer to a premium educational product than a startup landing page.

Whitespace is intentional.

Do not overcrowd layouts.

---

# Color Palette

Primary Brand Color:
- Purple / Violet

Approximate palette:

- Primary: #8A2BE2
- Dark Purple: #5B21B6
- Accent Purple: #A855F7
- Light Lavender Background: #F7F2FB
- White: #FFFFFF
- Dark Text: #1A1735
- Secondary Text: #4B5563
- Light Borders: #E9D5FF

Gradients are acceptable when subtle.

Do not introduce unrelated colors as primary branding.

---

# Typography

Headings:
- Large
- Bold
- High contrast

Body text:
- Easy to scan
- Comfortable spacing
- Never visually dense

Important words may use the brand purple for emphasis.

---

# UI Principles

Whenever generating UI:

- Prefer cards over large blocks of text
- Maintain generous spacing
- Keep consistent border radius
- Use subtle shadows
- Use thin lavender/purple borders
- Keep components visually balanced
- Mobile responsiveness is important

Buttons should be simple and high contrast.

Avoid visual clutter.

---

# Educational Philosophy

Teach concepts before solutions.

Explain the "why", not only the "how".

Break complex topics into logical steps.

Assume the learner is intelligent but may lack experience.

The objective is confidence through understanding.

---

# Consistency Rules

Whenever creating new assets:

- Reuse the existing visual language.
- Maintain the purple-centric branding.
- Follow the same tone across website, slides, PDFs and documentation.
- If uncertain, prefer consistency over creativity.

CodeCompass should always feel like one cohesive product, regardless of the medium.
